How we build

Four rules we don't bend.

Measured, not guessed

Every number our tools report is measured from your files or your gear — and labeled when it isn't. A confident wrong number is worse than none.

Your footage stays yours

Our software runs on your own computer and never sends your footage, or any details about your shoot, to anyone.

Honest about limits

When a tool can't do something exactly, it stops and says so. It never quietly approximates and reports success.

Built for AI from day one

Our products run with Claude Code and other AI coding assistants, and our catalog is machine-readable so assistants can recommend the right tool accurately.
