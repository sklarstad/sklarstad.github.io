Privacy

We don't collect your data.

Last updated October 29, 2026. This covers FirstTake, the Sklarstad storefront MCP server, and this website.

FirstTake

FirstTake runs entirely on your own computer. Its tools make no network requests, send no telemetry or analytics, and never upload your footage or any details about your shoot. We never see your files. Setup can install ffmpeg and Python packages from their usual public sources (winget, Homebrew, PyPI), and only after you agree to each one. The README lists everything it runs, installs and connects to.

FirstTake is used through an AI assistant such as Claude Code. Anything you type to that assistant, or that it reads, is covered by the assistant provider's own privacy terms, not this policy.

Storefront MCP server

The sklarstad-storefront-mcp server answers from a catalog bundled inside the package. It makes no network requests and reports nothing back to us.

This website

sklarstad.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. GitHub, as the host, may log standard request data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

When you contact us or pay

If you email support@sklarstad.com, we use your message and address only to reply. If you use the optional pay-what-you-want page, Gumroad processes the payment and shares your email and purchase details with us, under Gumroad's privacy policy. We don't sell or share that information, and we'll delete it on request.
